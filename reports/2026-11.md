# November 2026 Development Report

**Reporting period:** Oct 5 – Nov 6, 2026  
**Sources:** [#663](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/663), [#664](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/664), [#665](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/665), [#666](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/666), [#667](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/667), [#660](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/660), [#654](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/654), [#656](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/656), [#637](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/637), [#655](https://github.com/EqualifyEverything/equalify/issues/655)

## Executive Summary

The team shipped three platform releases this month (v2.1.1 on Oct 6, v2.1.2 on Oct 23, and v2.2.0 on Nov 2), closed out three long-running partner bug reports, and recorded 239 commits across 7 repositories. Scan reliability was the main theme. Version 2.1.2 fixed the large-audit timeouts reported by a partner in September, added an hourly sweep that closes out stalled scans and names the pages they missed, and stopped counting unresolved color contrast checks as blockers. Version 2.2.0 introduced audit tagging and consolidated every platform KPI into a single module with a daily Title II snapshot, which now feeds the admin Statistics panel, the Equalify Hub, and this report. The Iris PDF engine again accounted for most of the commit volume, with three Iris releases (v1.2.0 through v1.4.0) and three releases of the Iris WordPress plugin (0.2.1 through 0.3.0). No new bug reports or feature requests were filed against the main repository during the period.

## Highlights

- **Shipped three platform releases.** v2.1.1 put Scan History, the "What's New" notice area, and the refactored login screen into production. v2.1.2 delivered the scan reliability fixes. v2.2.0 delivered audit tagging and the unified KPI module.
- **Resolved the partner-reported large-audit timeouts.** The two affected audits turned out to have different problems: one had never started because its page list was too large for the hand-off to the scanning queue, and the other had scanned every page on every run but showed a false timeout notice when two scans overlapped. Both are fixed, and the partner confirmed on Oct 26.
- **Scan timeouts are now explicit.** A scan is only given up on when no page has reported back for a full hour. When that happens, the notice says how many pages were never scanned, each one is listed as "Not Scanned (Timeout)", and results from every page that did finish are kept.
- **Color contrast counts now match other tools.** Contrast checks that axe-core cannot resolve, such as text over images or gradients, are no longer reported as blockers. This closed a partner's June report of an 8,650-item discrepancy with axe Monitor.
- **Introduced audit tagging.** Users can create tags, assign them to audits, and filter the Audits screen by tag. Tags are shared across the organization, and admins can rename, merge, or delete them.
- **Unified platform KPIs with a daily Title II snapshot.** Every usage, adoption, conformance, and remediation figure is now computed in one place, with plain-language definitions published in the technical docs. The Equalify Hub's About page reads the same figures live.
- **Recorded presentations for the accessibility liaisons workshop**, extending outreach and training to campus partners.

Consolidated all platform KPIs into a single module with a daily Title II snapshot saved piece by piece, and added a public statistics endpoint for external dashboards. - **Iris PDF (equalify-iris-pdf).** The tagger now tags the page's own text in place (Oct 18), carries each equation as a Formula with its MathML attached and spoken alt text generated from it, and makes flat forms fillable from Iris's HTML (Oct 6). A new report-detail option prints the tag tree, each page's words and checks, field placement, and timing (Oct 25). PDF/UA-1 is now claimed only where veraPDF agrees, with a bounded font-width check so a crafted file cannot stall a run, and retagging clears old structure keys from form XObjects and patterns (Oct 30).
- **Iris for WordPress (equalify-iris-wp).** 0.2.1 hardened locking, saving, authentication, and HTTPS after review (Oct 5). 0.2.2 lets Iris count pages, caps its replies, and pauses on site copies (Oct 6). 0.3.0 places the tagged version at the PDF's original address (Oct 14). The repository also gained an automated PR reviewer and maintainer workflow, with CI serving the test site over HTTPS.
- **Iris QA (equalify-iris-qa).** A new repository received its license and the first HTML test pages for user testing of PDF-to-HTML decision points, contributed by Derek Riemer.
